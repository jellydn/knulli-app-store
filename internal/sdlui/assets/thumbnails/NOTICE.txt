Knulli App Store catalogue thumbnails

These images identify their respective projects. Their authors retain their
rights; inclusion does not imply endorsement. They are separate artwork, not
Store-authored logos. The Store bundles thumbnails for offline browsing and
does not fetch images, inspect installed files or execute publisher code.

Changes: resize to fit 80 x 80, center on a transparent 80 x 80 canvas and strip
image metadata with ImageMagick. Aspect ratios are retained; images are not
cropped or recolored. Modified for the Store on 2026-11-03. Example
transformation, with the original source paths listed below:

  magick SOURCE -thumbnail 80x80 -background none -gravity center \
    -extent 80x80 -strip PACKAGE_ID.png

Fallbacks

Random Game Picker, Handheld Link Cores, retroshelf, and unknown packages use
Store-rendered initials, not publisher logos. No unverified image is copied.

Device packages append this notice and all the accompanying licence texts to
the existing managed README.txt, so self-updates retain the notices without
adding a new write path. The Store's MIT licence does not replace these
separately licensed images or imply rights to a project's trademarks.
